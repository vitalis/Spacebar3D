# Changelog

## v1.0.0

- Preview 3D models in Finder with Quick Look or open them in standalone viewer windows. Reopen recent files or set Spacebar 3D as the default app for supported formats.
- Open VRM, VRMA, glTF, GLB, FBX, OBJ, STL, 3MF, PLY, USD, USDA, USDC, USDZ, 3DS and Blender files. View point clouds in OBJ and PLY files.
- Inspect models in Solid, Textured or Normal view. Overlay a wireframe, check the topology and the face and vertex counts, switch between flat and smooth shading, and adjust metallic and roughness values.
- Apply Unshaded, Anime, Cel-Shaded, Retro, Voxel Art or Hologram styles.
- Orbit, pan, zoom to fit, align the camera to an axis, show a floor grid or rotate the model automatically. Small Finder previews show the model only.
- Choose from seven lighting moods, adjust brightness and direction, or rotate the light automatically. Floor shadows and reflections show where the model sits.
- Select animation clips, scrub through motion and repeat playback. Preview supported animation-only FBX and VRMA files on a skeleton.
- View VRM avatars with toon shading, outlines and spring-bone motion.
- Load embedded textures and grant read-only access to a model folder for external textures, materials and geometry. Textured view is disabled when none of the referenced textures can be drawn.
- Set defaults for the opening view or style, background and solid colors, brightness, grid, rotation and animation playback.
- Navigate with keyboard commands, context menus and VoiceOver actions. The viewer supports Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast.
- Open the viewer's Help menu in the app or Quick Look for controls, acknowledgements and the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md). In the app, **Help → Spacebar 3D Support** opens the project's issue tracker. Models are processed locally.
- Use the interface in English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Korean, Italian or Russian.
