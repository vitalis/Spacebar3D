# Spacebar 3D Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Spacebar 3D and its Finder Quick Look extension process 3D models on your Mac. Neither sends your models, textures, file names, or viewing activity to the developer or to third parties. Spacebar 3D has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## Files you open

When you open or preview a model, Spacebar 3D reads the file to display its geometry, materials, and animations. Some models reference textures or other files stored separately. If you grant access to a folder, the viewer can read related files in that folder and its subfolders. Access is read-only.

## Information kept on your Mac

- **Recent models.** The app remembers up to ten recently opened models using file references that let you reopen them. It also tells macOS about opened files for the system’s recent-document menus. Choose **File → Open Recent → Clear Menu** to clear both recent-document lists.
- **Folder permissions.** The app saves references to folders you authorize so it can load related files on later visits. It shares folder references locally with its Quick Look extension. Clearing recent models leaves these separate folder permissions in place.
- **Preferences.** The app saves viewer settings and window placement on your Mac. The Quick Look extension reads the shared viewer settings so previews use your choices. Choose **Restore Defaults** in Settings to reset those viewer preferences.

The developer receives no copy of these local records and has no server-side records of your model use to retain, share, or delete.
